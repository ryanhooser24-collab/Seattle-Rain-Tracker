Max per fill

Max per ticker
Total dollars across all fills on one market — caps the walk-up loop
Min fill
Smallest allowed fill — a sized fill below this stops the loop. Must be ≤ max per fill or nothing trades.
City whitelist
Only these cities may receive real orders. Comma-separated; empty = all cities.
Sizing — how much money per bet
Bankroll unitiKelly sizing base in $ — every stake scales linearly with this. THE knob for scaling risk: double this = double every bet. Raise in 2× steps and watch slippage.
Kelly multiplier lockediSafety discount for model overconfidence. Locked at 0.50 — model probs run ~20pts hot on selected bets; raising this overbets. Scale bankroll unit instead.
Max positionsiTotal open positions allowed at once. Rarely binds — leave at 10 unless the whitelist grows past 8 cities.
Max per cityiOpen positions allowed per city. Caps correlated risk — same city, same weather miss. 2 is right while proving out.
Entries — what gets bought
Min gradeiOnly signals at this grade or better trade. A only — B-grades were negative EV in every backtest.
Momentum entriesiWhen a fresh forecast run jumps the model prob but the market hasn't repriced, boost the signal one grade. Backtest: 56% win vs 41% baseline at the same price.
Momentum jumpiMin model prob jump (0–1) since last scan to trigger. 0.15 backtested best. Lower = more triggers, weaker signal.
Momentum max ask moveiMax ask rise ($) since last scan to still count as "market lagging". If the ask already moved, the edge is gone. 0.02 backtested.
Exits — when to cut a position
ExitsiSell the whole position at bid when the model gives up on it. Backtest: +57% EV improvement — the market reprices hours after the model. Leave on.
Exit thresholdiExit when current prob < this × prob at entry. 0.60 backtested best (0.3–0.7 all beat holding). Higher = faster exits, more false exits.
Scanner
Scan intervaliSeconds between market scans. 300 = 5 min. Faster burns API quota for little gain; slower misses momentum windows.
